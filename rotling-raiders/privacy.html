Rotling Raiders Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Rotling Raiders is a game for players of all ages. We built it to collect no personal information at all.

What we collect

Nothing. Rotling Raiders has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. It does not use the internet to send anything about you or your device.

What stays on your device

Your base, coins, rebirth level, daily-spin day and sound setting are stored in the game's own storage. This is saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. There are no chats, no links to social media and no purchases in the game.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add optional purchases), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Open a request on our support page.
