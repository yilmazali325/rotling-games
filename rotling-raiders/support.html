Common questions

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to walk. Buy friends from the moving belt - they earn coins every second. Sneak into rival bases to steal theirs, run home, and lock your door to keep yours safe.

What is Rebirth?

When you have enough coins you can start over on a new island. You lose your friends and coins, but everything earns more forever.

What is the daily spin?

One free spin a day for coins, a free friend or double coins for a while.

How do I turn off the sound?

Tap the SOUND button at the top right.
