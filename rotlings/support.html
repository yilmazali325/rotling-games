Common questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere in the jar to drop a friend. Drop it onto its twin and the two merge into a bigger friend. Don't let the pile go above the dashed line!

What is a level?

Each level asks you to make one creature within a number of drops. Use fewer drops to earn more stars and coins.

How do I turn off the sound?

Tap the SOUND button at the top of the screen.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
