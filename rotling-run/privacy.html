Rotling Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Rotling Run is a runner game for players aged 13 and up. We built it to collect no personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Rotling Run has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services of our own. It does not send anything about you or your device to us.

Purchases

The game offers optional coin packs and a Coin Doubler, behind a parent gate. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email or payment details; the game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was completed so it can add your coins.

What stays on your device

Your coins, levels, stars, upgrades, outfits, trophies and settings are stored in the game's own storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Rotling Run is rated for ages 12-13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no chats and no links to social media.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the store privacy details before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Open a request on our support page.
