Rotling Shop Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Rotling Shop is a game for players of all ages. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your name, email or anything else about you.

What the game sends to our server

Rotling Shop has a Raid feature where you sneak into other players' toy shops and they can sneak into yours. To make that work, the game talks to our own game server and stores:

  • A random player ID. The first time you play, the server makes up an ID and a shop name for you (like "Ravioli Rana's Toy Corner"). It is not your name and is not connected to you, your device's advertising ID or any other app.
  • Your shop. Your shop level, coins, whether you have a guard and how many plushies are on each shelf, so other players can raid a copy of it.
  • Raid results. Who raided whom, whether they got away and how many coins changed hands, so we can show "you got raided" and offer revenge.

That is all. This data is used only to run the Raid feature. It is never sold, shared with anyone, or used for ads or tracking. Like any website, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as the internet address a request came from) to keep the server working and secure.

What stays on your device

Your coins, upgrades, shopper book, lesson progress and sound setting are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no chats, no messages, no photos, no friend lists, no links to social media and no purchases. Other players only ever see your made-up shop name and your shop's plushies.

Deleting your data

Shops that are not played for 14 days are no longer shown to other players. To have your player ID and shop removed from our server completely, open a request on our support page with your shop name (shown when someone raids you) and we will delete it.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add optional purchases), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Open a request on our support page.
